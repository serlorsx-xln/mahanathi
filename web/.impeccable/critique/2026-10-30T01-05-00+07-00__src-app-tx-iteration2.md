## 2. Design specificity verdict

This is authored for this product and nothing else. The signature mark — 804 slim instanced gauges whose geometry encodes the data before colour does (bank-full cap at 78% of the pole, water fill inside, overflow rising above the cap in the alert red, stale stations reduced to a quiet cap-only stroke) — is a hydrographic instrument, not a 3D-template prop, and the shader carries the NW-key/Imhof lighting, paddy flatness toning, neighbour desaturation and aerial fog that the brief's world demands (Terrain.tsx). The type pairing is decided on measured evidence (signed-figure alignment, ink-vs-ascender, line-box use — ADR 0005), the situation scale was found by constrained CVD search with delta-E evidence replayed live on /color, and the national sentence "ตอนนี้ 18 สถานีน้ำล้นตลิ่ง ส่วนใหญ่อยู่ที่นครปฐม กาญจนบุรี และสุพรรณบุรี" is the typographic centrepiece exactly as specified. Nothing here could be transplanted to an unrelated product unchanged; the only category-generic surfaces are the dev evidence pages, which are deliberately quiet one-column ruled documents. Iteration 1's fixes (base.css theming, error path, honest ใกล้ฉัน annotation, blur discipline) landed and are verified in source and tests.

## 3. Remaining issues

**[P2-1] The no-WebGL floor announces "loading" forever after the data has loaded — a regression from the iteration-1 skeleton fix.**
- What: `Lookdev.tsx` — the canvas branch is `data && webgl ? <Scene/> : !failed ? <skeleton role="status">…</> : null`. When WebGL is unavailable and the fetch succeeds, `data && webgl` is false and `!failed` is true, so the sweeping skeleton and its screen-reader status persist indefinitely. Pre-fix this branch was a silent `<div>`; iteration 1 added `role="status"` + SR text to it, turning a cosmetic leftover into a false, never-resolving announcement.
- Why it matters: a screen-reader visitor on the no-WebGL floor is told the map is loading for as long as they stay; the infinite sweep also runs forever.
- Fix: gate the loading branch on `!data`; extend the no-WebGL e2e test to assert the skeleton is absent once the panel is visible.

**[P2-2] Gauge level-of-detail (brief §4: "As you zoom in, gauges grow and gain labels") is not implemented and is not on the accepted-deferral list.**
- Fix: ship a first LOD step now, or record the deferral explicitly in the gate doc.

**[P3-1] Retry gives no feedback while in flight.** Clear `failed` when `attempt` changes, so the loading status returns.
**[P3-2] 84 hollow stale gauges have no on-page key on /lookdev.** Append a sixth, textFaint-coloured entry "ยังไม่อัปเดต 84" to the numbers row.
**[P3-3] Inconsistent missing-value markers.** `selected.wl?.toFixed(3)` renders empty vs `'·'` elsewhere. Unify.
**[P3-4] Terminology drift for the bank ratio.** "สัดส่วนตลิ่ง" vs "% ของตลิ่ง" — pick the plainer one.
**[P3-5] Active preset is visual-only.** No `aria-pressed`/`aria-current` on the preset buttons.
**[P3-6] Mobile: the station sheet's bottom edge tucks under the dev bar.** Dev-route cosmetic; revisit with Phase 4's real bottom sheet.
**[P3-7] Dead token.** `--shadow-panel` referenced nowhere after iteration 1 dropped the panel shadow. Use in Phase 4 or delete.

**Copy check against §8:** all pass. The error copy names the problem and the way out in the brief's own example pattern, with a working, e2e-tested retry.
